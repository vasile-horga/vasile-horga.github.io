About

Vasile Horga is an Associate Professor at the Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași, Romania, within the Department of Energy Utilisation, Electrical Drives and Industrial Automation.

This website presents selected research themes and the scientific output documented in the supplied publication list. Additional biographical information and contact details can be added when verified.

This is an initial version of the academic website. No photograph or unverified personal details have been included.

SCIENTIFIC INTERESTS

Research

Research themes reflected in the listed publications and patents.

01

Electrical drives and motor control

Control strategies for induction, synchronous and permanent-magnet machines.

02

System identification and estimation

Continuous-time modelling, parameter estimation and self-commissioning methods.

03

Electric and hybrid vehicles

Electric propulsion, hybrid powertrains and distributed control using CAN/CANopen.

04

Robotics and automation

Omnidirectional mobile robots, motion control and virtual laboratories.

05

Power electronics and digital control

Converters, PWM techniques, DSP/FPGA-based implementations and drive systems.

06

Energy-efficient control

Optimal and adaptive control of electrical drive systems.
